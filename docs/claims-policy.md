# VoltSure AI — Scientific Honesty & Claims Policy

**Non-Negotiable Product Safeguards:**

### 1. Phrasing of Probability
- **Never claim:** *"Guaranteed to make it"*, *"100% arrival certainty"*, or *"Zero chance of stalling"*.
- **Standardized phrasing:** *"Estimated probability of arriving with reserve intact, under stated data and model assumptions."*

### 2. Vehicle Profiles & OEM Disclaimers
- Reference vehicles (such as the Ola S1 Z reference) appear exclusively as illustrative profiles.
- Standard notice: *"Claimed figures from a user-supplied reference; not independently validated. No OEM partnership or endorsement is claimed."*

### 3. Charger Status & OCPP Adapters
- Standard notice: *"OCPP-compatible adapter interface; operator support required; demo uses a labelled simulator. No commercial CPO relationship or live grid control is claimed."*

### 4. Calibration vs Demo Isolation
- Scenario demonstrations in the UI are isolated from benchmark calibration evidence.
- A single successful trip is never presented as proof of statistical calibration.
- Every number shown on screen originates from a pure TypeScript engine calculation (`src/engine/`), never static hardcoded copy.
